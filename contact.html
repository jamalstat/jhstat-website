What happens next

  1. Your enquiry is read and acknowledged, normally within two working days.
  2. If it looks like a good fit, a short scoping call is arranged — free of charge.
  3. You receive a written proposal covering scope, deliverables, timeline and cost.
  4. Nothing is chargeable until you have agreed that proposal in writing.

Before you write

Please do not send patient-identifiable data, unpublished results or other confidential material in a first email. Once an engagement is agreed, a data-sharing or non-disclosure agreement and an appropriate secure transfer route will be put in place.

Common questions

Before you get in touch

Is the first conversation really free?

Yes. An initial scoping conversation of around 30 minutes is free and without obligation. It exists so both sides can work out whether the project is a good fit before anyone commits time or money.

Is my project too small?

Probably not. Short pieces of work — a sample size calculation, a review of an analysis plan, a second opinion on a reviewer's statistical comment — are common and welcome.

I am a doctoral researcher. Can you help?

Yes, with methodological and statistical support of the kind a university statistical service or supervisor would provide. JH Stat does not complete assessed academic work on a student's behalf or provide support that would breach your institution's academic integrity rules. Please discuss any external statistical support with your supervisor first — JH Stat is happy to correspond with them directly to confirm the scope of what is provided.

Can you work under our institution's contract and governance rules?

Yes. Purchase orders, supplier onboarding, non-disclosure agreements, data-sharing agreements and information governance requirements are all routine. Please mention any procurement process in your enquiry so it can be factored into the timeline.

Do you work with organisations outside the UK?

Yes, where time zones and any data transfer requirements can be accommodated. Say where you are based in your enquiry.
